Frequently Asked Questions.

Why wouldn't I just use a template? +
Templates look like templates. Your client's brand deserves more than a Canva export. This is directed creative — designed to their specific brand, goals, and audience. Not a fill-in-the-blank.
Does my client need a copy deck? +
Yes — I need some form of tone and content direction from the client so the emails read like their brand, not mine. A full copy deck is ideal, but even basic voice guidelines, sample copy, or a few "this sounds like us" examples will work. If the client doesn't have anything, the Brand Interpretation add-on can help establish that foundation.
What if my client doesn't have a brand system? +
That's what the Brand Interpretation add-on is for. I can do a light creative pass (color, type, voice direction) or build out full brand guidelines. Quoted per engagement.
5 days — is that real? +
5 business days from the moment I have the client's brand assets in hand. The clock starts at asset handoff, not the intro. The faster assets land, the faster work ships. This isn't a side project — the process is dialed.
What do I need to provide? +
Brand assets (logo, colors, fonts — whatever exists), a copy deck or tone-of-voice direction, the campaign goal, and a 30-minute call with the client. I handle everything else.
How do revisions work? +
I present the work and we do one round of revisions together. The work is directed from the start, so we're typically aligned by delivery.
Do you handle the platform upload? +
I deliver clean, production-ready HTML files. The client or their team uploads to their ESP. The code is built to work anywhere — no platform-specific quirks.
